  selection or tokenizer/default checks. Existing TP2 rank scripts keep working unchanged.

  default and Windows pipe-helper changes. `src/program/generate.cpp`, `src/ds4/ds4_model.cu`
  and `tools/ds4/slot_keep_test.py` stay exact to the fork base. Resolve the installer conflict
  by adding only `pack_index_files`; do not reintroduce previously excluded Metal functions.
- Keep fork-owned README/AGENTS and numeric VERSION1.1.42. Merge applicable catalog/settings
